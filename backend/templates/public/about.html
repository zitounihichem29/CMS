WHO WE ARE

What is ORSC?

The Operations Research Society Club (ORSC) is the student community for Operations Research and Mathematics at USTHB.

We are building a strong, proud, and motivated community where Mathematics and Operations Research students feel central, connected, and valued.

Our goal is to help students believe in themselves, discover the power of analytical thinking, and see how Mathematics and Operations Research can create real-world impact.

02
OUR STORY

Our History

Originally founded in May 1990 under the name “Club Recherche Opérationnelle”, the club has built a long history within USTHB.

Over the years, it earned several awards and distinctions, including being named the second-best club at the university in 2016.

In 2019, the club was relaunched by a new team and rebranded as ORSC, with a fresh identity and a renewed ambition to connect Operations Research with today’s real-world challenges.

DIGITAL LEGACY 2025 — 2026

Built for the Club. Designed to Outlive a Mandate.

The ORSC digital platform was created during a period of renewal and restructuring within the Operations Research Society Club.

As the club rebuilt its internal organization, activities, and long-term vision, the need for a structured digital environment became increasingly important. The platform was developed to transform that vision into a lasting digital system capable of supporting both the club's public presence and its internal operations.

More than a public website, it provides the foundation of a complete digital management system for ORSC, bringing together member management, recruitment, departments, tasks, events, internal documents, activity tracking, institutional records, and the preservation of the club's history.

The project was designed not for a single team or mandate, but as a long-term digital foundation for the club. Its structure is intended to evolve with ORSC, allowing future teams to maintain it, improve it, and expand its capabilities over time.

Behind this platform stands a broader effort to rebuild, organize, and strengthen ORSC — an effort shaped by the members who contributed their time, ideas, leadership, and technical work to creating a stronger foundation for the club's future.

OUR PURPOSE

Our Mission

Our mission is to share knowledge and enthusiasm for Operations Research while developing problem-solving, analytical, and collaborative skills.

We aim to create a community of students who can transform mathematical thinking into practical solutions and meaningful impact.

OUR DIRECTION

4 Strategic Pillars

01

Advance and Elevate Operations Research

Promote OR and make its value more visible to students.

02

Bridge the Gap to Professional Careers

Connect students with researchers, professionals, companies, and real opportunities.

03

Translate Theory into Practice

Turn academic knowledge into projects, challenges, and real-world applications.

04

Provide Academic and Peer Support

Create an environment where students can learn, exchange knowledge, and grow together.

WHERE WE ARE GOING

Our Vision

Our vision is to build a generation of students who are confident in their abilities, passionate about Operations Research, and capable of applying analytical thinking to real-world challenges.

We want ORSC to become a strong bridge between university learning, research, innovation, and professional life.
